star Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password if I forget it? expand_more
You can reset your password by clicking 'Forgot Password' on the login screen. We will send an OTP to your registered mobile number for verification.
Where can I check the status of my application? expand_more
Navigate to the 'Applications' tab on the left menu (or bottom bar on mobile). There you will see a list of all your active and past applications along with their current status.
What documents do I need for a scheme? expand_more
Required documents vary by scheme. Typically, you will need your Aadhar Card, Ration Card, and bank details. Specific requirements are listed on each scheme's details page.
